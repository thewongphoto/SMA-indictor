01 / RECOMMENDED DIRECTION

A home that meets you where you are.

Home → Choose securityOne primary action. Submit a partial ticker or name to search.
Parameters → Your resultOne visible preference. Advanced detail stays folded.
Home → Saved line → ChartReturning users skip search and parameters.

Teach the next decision

An optional sample explains SMA and upward crossings. Small explanations sit beside the frequency choice and chart. Learn stays available for replay; no mandatory tour.

Keep Home quiet

New users see a starting point. Returning users see their saved company groups. Detailed metrics, strategy comparisons and notification controls live deeper in the app.

Jump to the visual flow maps ↓

Try the phone: start an analysis, submit “app”, select Apple, adjust the frequency, save the example result and return Home. Or switch to “Returning user” and tap View.
