Notes. Chart lines are sample data. Emailing the developer on each email save or change needs one small server endpoint that both the Android app and the iPhone web app call; the apps should not hold mail credentials. Backtest amounts, dates and the connection status are sample values, not real results. Signal copy is plain and avoids profit claims, per the app's research-only disclaimer. Status badges pair words with colour. The Charts tab reuses the SMA detail screen. Dark is shown; a light variant follows the system setting once you confirm the layout.
